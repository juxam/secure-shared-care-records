# Research Scope

## In Scope

This project examines cross-provider access to patient medical records for direct care. The care contexts include GP surgeries, ambulance services, emergency departments, urgent treatment centres, walk-in clinics, secondary care providers and specialist services.

The project considers the security, privacy, governance and trust implications of enabling healthcare professionals to view fuller or complete patient records when providing care.

## Out of Scope

The project does not primarily examine:

- Secondary use of health data for research.
- Commercial use of patient data.
- AI training on patient records.
- Population-level analytics.
- Genomic data sharing, unless directly relevant to shared record access.
- Full technical implementation of an EHR platform.

## Core Assumption

The project assumes that improved record accessibility can support better care delivery, but only if access is justified, proportionate, secure, auditable and transparent.

## Central Tension

The central tension is between clinical availability and privacy/security proportionality. Clinicians need the right information at the right time, but patients also need assurance that their sensitive health information is not unnecessarily exposed.
