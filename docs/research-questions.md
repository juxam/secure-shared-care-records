# Research Questions

## Main Research Question

What security and privacy implications arise from enabling cross-provider access to complete patient medical records for direct care across GP, ambulance, urgent care, emergency care, secondary care, walk-in and specialist-care settings?

## Sub-Questions

RQ1. What patient-record information is needed across different direct-care contexts?

RQ2. What security risks arise when multiple healthcare organisations and professional roles access shared patient records?

RQ3. What privacy risks arise from wider visibility of complete or near-complete patient records?

RQ4. How do current governance, data protection and NHS assurance mechanisms address these risks?

RQ5. What initial risk taxonomy can support the design of secure, privacy-preserving shared patient-record access?
