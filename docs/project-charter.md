# Project Charter

## Working Title

Secure Shared Care Records: Security and Privacy Implications of Cross-Provider Access to Complete Patient Medical Records

## Project Summary

This project investigates the security and privacy implications of enabling cross-provider access to complete patient medical records across GP surgeries, ambulance services, urgent and emergency care, walk-in clinics, secondary care and specialist providers.

The project examines how wider record accessibility can support safer, faster and more coordinated care while preserving confidentiality, proportionality, accountability, auditability, security and patient trust.

## Research Aim

To investigate the security and privacy implications of cross-provider access to complete patient medical records for direct care and to develop an evidence-informed framework for secure, privacy-preserving and clinically useful shared record access.

## Core Problem

Healthcare professionals often require timely access to patient information to provide safe and effective care. However, wider access to complete records increases the risk of unauthorised access, insider misuse, over-disclosure, weak accountability, poor auditability, supplier exposure, patient mistrust and non-compliance with data protection and information governance requirements.

## Project Boundary

This project focuses on access to patient records for direct care across healthcare settings. It does not primarily focus on secondary uses of data such as research analytics, AI model training, population health management, commercial data use or public health surveillance.

## Primary Care Settings Covered

- GP surgeries
- Ambulance services
- Emergency departments
- Urgent treatment centres
- Walk-in clinics
- Secondary care providers
- Specialist providers
- Community care interfaces where relevant

## Expected Outputs

1. A policy and literature-based risk taxonomy.
2. A stakeholder-informed understanding of trust, access and accountability.
3. A context-aware security and privacy risk assessment framework.
4. A GitHub-hosted set of reusable research artefacts, synthetic scenarios, templates and toolkit components.
