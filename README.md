# Secure Shared Care Records

This repository supports a research programme investigating the security and privacy implications of cross-provider access to complete patient medical records across GP surgeries, ambulance services, urgent and emergency care, walk-in clinics, secondary care and specialist providers.

The project examines how complete patient-record access can support safer and more coordinated care while preserving confidentiality, proportionality, accountability, auditability and patient trust.

## Research Aim

To investigate the security and privacy implications of cross-provider access to complete patient medical records for direct care and to develop an evidence-informed framework for secure, privacy-preserving and clinically useful shared record access.

## Research Outputs

1. A policy and literature-based risk taxonomy.
2. A stakeholder-informed model of trust, access and accountability.
3. A context-aware security and privacy risk assessment framework.
4. Reusable templates, synthetic scenarios and research artefacts.
